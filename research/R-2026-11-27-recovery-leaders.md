**Idea.** Know which groups have to lead for a bounce to be the real thing.

**How it was tested.** For each start date, median 10-session return per sub-theme (split-adjusted closes, day before the signal → day 10),
ranked as a percentile across sub-themes. Averaged over the 4 real starts and over the 3 failed ones.

**Result.** Real turns: the AI-hardware chain (power/analog semis, memory, semicap, optical, neoclouds, data-center power) plus
risk-on beta (copper, bitcoin miners, digital banks/fintech, rare earths). Failed turns: defensive and "safe" groups led (immunology biotech,
gold majors, consumer brands, medical devices, utilities, hotels/cruise). Gold mid-tiers ranked high in both, so gold leading says nothing.

**Caveats.** 7 events. Every real recovery so far was an AI-led market; a recovery led by something new would not look like this.

**How it could be used.** A "who's leading the bounce" check on the Post-thrust board: share of the real-recovery groups vs the defensive
groups in the top 20 sub-themes over the first 10 sessions.
