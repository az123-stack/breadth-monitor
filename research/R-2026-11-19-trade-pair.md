**Idea.** The Scoreboard's "+32%" uses today's baskets over the same 20 sessions that chose them, so it is hindsight. Test the
version you could have placed: pick the baskets with data up to the entry day, long the winning fifth, short the losing fifth, equal weight.

**Result.** Positive in both halves but not proven (t 1.2–1.7). About half the edge is the long side (+1.7% vs the average group at 20d),
half the short side. The Oct 21, 2026 entry worked (+20%) almost entirely through the short leg (Bitcoin miners, neoclouds, quantum,
drones, rare earths, uranium collapsed); the winners went nowhere. Of today's 25 losing groups, 18 were already losers on Oct 21;
of today's 26 winners only 9 were winners then (the long side rotated, the short side persisted).

**Caveats.** ~20 independent windows, no borrow costs or squeezes (shorting junk baskets is where a −23% month came from),
today's sub-theme lists, survivorship. Information only.
