**Idea.** Adam saw cybersecurity (ZS, NTSK, CHKP, CRWD) stepping up together after pulling back into their EMAs and wanted
to see it coming: (a) stocks nuzzled into their 9/21 EMAs, (b) stocks breaking the top of their last short-term step,
(c) whole sub-themes positioned together so he knows a group is setting up for a move.

**How it was tested.** Daily bars, split-adjusted. Stock states: uptrend (21 EMA > 50 EMA, 50 rising over 10 sessions);
nuzzle (stepped up first = closed 2+ ADRs over the 21 EMA within 20 sessions, then low into the 9/21 ribbon, close not more
than 1.5 ADR under the 21 or 1 ADR above the ribbon); lift (nuzzled in the prior 5 sessions, close over the 9 EMA and the
prior high); step break (first close over the prior 15-session high after a nuzzle in that window). Group: setting up = 50%+
of liquid members nuzzled in 5 sessions (half in uptrend); stepping up = 40%+ lifted or broke in 3 sessions after 40%+ were
nuzzled 3 sessions earlier. Outcome: group equal-weight index vs SPY from the NEXT close over 10/20 sessions, first day of
each state (one per 10 sessions), compared with the average group and with the average top-30% (60-day) group on the same
dates; t on date averages; split halves by date. Independent recompute with median-member returns and its own 60-day
leader rank: setting up +1.75% (t 1.73, halves +0.3/+3.3), stepping up +1.85% (t 1.95, halves +1.0/+3.8), breaks ≈ 0.

**Result.** On its own the group state is noise: setting up +1.4% vs the average group but median −0.6% (a few groups that
ripped carry the mean), stepping up +0.8% (median −0.7%). The useful part is the pullback inside groups that already lead:
setting up together in a top-30% group beat other top-30% groups by ~+2% over 20 sessions with both halves positive;
stepping up together ~+1.7%. Pure step breaks (no reclaim) were the weakest (−1.1% main calc, ≈ 0 recompute): chasing the
breakout bar adds nothing. Weak breadth hurt every variant (setting up ≥ 70% in weak breadth −1.6% next 10, t −2.8).
Oct 23, 2026: Cybersecurity Platforms stepping up together day 3 (ZS, NTSK, RBRK step breaks; CRWD, FTNT lifting off
the EMAs; 9 of 9 checks), 8 groups stepping up, 11 setting up.

**Caveats.** t just under / at 2, so not proven; ~2 years, bull-heavy, survivors only, today's sub-theme lists, no costs;
most of the raw gain is plain group momentum (leading groups +1.7% vs average). Thresholds were set by eye on cyber and not
tuned to the backtest.

**How it could be used.** Built as information only (Rotation tab "Stair steps" card, Unproven tag): groups stepping /
setting up with a 15-session strip so the coil and go is visible across sessions, plus a stocks view with nuzzled names
first (9/21 EMA pick-up levels, distance to the step high). Could later feed the pick context (a nuzzled stock in a leading
group setting up) if a forward test holds t ≥ 2. Re-test as more history arrives.
