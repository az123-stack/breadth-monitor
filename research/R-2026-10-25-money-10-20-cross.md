**Idea.** Adam: money left the market over the last two weeks (Breadth tab "Money in or out" chart, 10-day average of net
dollar volume under the dashed 20-day). Plan: stay hands off until the 10-day crosses back above the 20-day.

**How it was tested.** `dv_net` from data/breadth.csv (rising minus falling $ volume, $B), 10- and 20-day simple averages as
drawn on the chart. State known at the close; outcome = SPY and the equal-weight average of liquid stocks ($5+, $20M+/day,
clipped ±50%) from the NEXT open to the close 5/10/21 sessions later. Split halves by date.

**Result.** The cross is not a gate. The 10-day sat under the 20-day 56% of the time and flipped 40 times in 20 months
(a stretch lasts ~6 sessions). Forward SPY and stock returns were the same or slightly BETTER while it was under; the cross-up
day itself had no edge. The deepest outflows (10-day average in its bottom fifth, where Oct 23 sits at the 11th percentile,
−$53B vs −$17B on the 20-day; 10-day share in falling stocks 53.6%) were followed by above-average returns in both halves,
in line with the comeback study (snapbacks start around heavy-selling days).

**Follow-up: wait for the cross after a deep outflow?** (`tools/money_cross_wait.py`) 17 episodes where the 10-day was under
the 20-day AND in its bottom fifth (first day of each), Feb 2025 – Sep 2026. Cross came back after 6 sessions on average (1–12).
SPY during the wait +0.03% on average (low point −1.3% avg, worst −7.5% Feb/Apr 2025). SPY next 21 sessions: buy now +1.64% vs
buy at the cross +1.61%; average liquid stock +0.45% vs −0.16%. Waiting neither helped nor hurt on average: it dodged the dip in
Apr 2025 / Mar 2026 (then +12% / +11% after the cross) but not the Feb–Mar 2025 decline (−3.1% after the cross). Patience rule, not edge.

**Caveats.** ~2 years, bull-heavy (the one real bear, Feb–Apr 2025, is in sample), overlapping windows, survivors only, no
costs. Says nothing about which setups work; that lives in the playbook (post-thrust vs breadth regime) and box study
(springs did better when money was leaving, +0.21R).

**How it could be used.** Not as an on/off switch. Keep the chart as description. Defensive posture should come from the
playbook state (no thrust window + weak breadth = half size, A-grade only), not this cross.
