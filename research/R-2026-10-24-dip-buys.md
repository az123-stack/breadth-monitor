**Idea.** Adam likes the Box and Strength on Weakness names and wants to time entries on a wick under the daily 9 or
21 EMA that comes back up, plus see which of those names are sitting under the 9 / 21 right now, live from Google.

**How it was tested.** Every liquid RS 70+ stock in an uptrend, every day. Events: low under the EMA and close at or above
it (wick & back), close under the 9 but over the 21, close under the 21, reclaim (closed under in one of the prior 3 sessions,
back over now). Entry next open, exit N sessions later minus SPY; compared with all uptrend stock-days on the same date (date
averages, t on the daily edge, halves split by date). R view: stop at the wick low (or 1 ADR) with risk floored at 0.3 ADR.
Box / SoW membership history isn't stored, so this is the generic version of the pattern, not on Adam's lists.

**Result.** No edge. A wick-and-back day did the same as any other uptrend day; closing under the 9 while holding the 21 was
slightly worse at 5 sessions (t −2.5, halves both negative but 10-day not significant). The wick-low stop keeps risk small
but the win rate drops (25% vs 32%), so R is unchanged.

**Caveats.** Generic universe, not Adam's Box / SoW names; bull-heavy 2 years; survivors only; no costs; daily bars (no
intraday path, so "wick then back" means the close was back over the EMA).

**How it could be used.** Built as information only: a "Dip buys · 9 / 21 EMA" card at the top of Trade Setups and on the
Intraday tab listing Box longs + SoW names wicked under & back / under now, live from the Google Sheet (new column L "low").
It's an entry-location tool on names already picked by Box / SoW and the ★ group, never a reason on its own. Could be re-tested
on the Box / SoW signals themselves (e.g. retest entries filled on a 9/21 wick vs at the next open) once there is time.
